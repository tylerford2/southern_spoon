Southern Flavors for Your Special Day!

Since 2008, Southern Spoon has been redefining southern cuisine for couples and families in Nashville, TN. We specialize in turning classic southern favorites into unforgettable dishes that make your big day truly special.

Led by Chef Adam Barnes, our team combines a love for southern flavors with fresh, modern techniques to create a menu that's uniquely yours. From intimate gatherings to grand celebrations, we're here to bring warmth, flavor, and creativity to your wedding or event.

At Southern Spoon, every meal is made with heart. Let us help make your special moments even more delicious.
