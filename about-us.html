About Southern Spoon

Southern comfort food, made by people who love Nashville.

Our Owners

Adam & Crystal Barnes

Southern Spoon is owned and operated by Chef Adam Barnes and Crystal Barnes. What started in 2008 as a single food truck has grown into a full-scale wedding and corporate catering company — but the mission hasn't changed: bring people together over food that feels like home.

Adam leads the kitchen, developing a Southern fusion menu that takes classic dishes and gives them a creative, modern spin. Together, Adam and Crystal have built Southern Spoon into the team Nashville couples and companies trust with their biggest days.

Our Mission & Vision

Southern Fusion, Handcrafted With Heart

Southern Spoon began in 2008 as a single food truck and has grown into a full catering company serving weddings, corporate events, and private gatherings across Nashville — without losing the from-scratch, made-with-heart approach we started with.

Every menu blends time-honored Southern flavors with fresh, modern technique — think grazing tables, fried pickled okra, red-eye wellington, and sweet potato cream cheese wontons. Menus are seasonal and regularly updated to reflect what's fresh throughout the year.

Meet Our Food Truck

Jarvis

Jarvis is a fully equipped mobile kitchen ready to tackle large events and can travel anywhere. At 30 feet long and weighing nearly 10 tons, Jarvis is a commercial-grade operation — not your typical backyard setup.
