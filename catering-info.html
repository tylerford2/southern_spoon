Catering Info

Southern Spoon offers catering for weddings, corporate events, and everything in between.

Events We Cater

Weddings, Corporate Gatherings, and Everything In Between

From engagement parties and wedding receptions to corporate lunches, product launches, and private dinners at home, we build the menu and service style around your event — not the other way around. Below is a look at how we typically show up.

Food Truck

Our mobile kitchen is perfect for any event and is increasingly becoming popular for birthdays and weddings.

Minimum reservation: $2,000 (covers approx. 50–60 people)

Wedding Events

Engagement parties, rehearsal dinners, and wedding receptions.

Starts at $25 per person

Corporate Events

Team meetings, product launches, employee appreciation, and customer appreciation.

Starts at $20 per person

Grazing Tables

Free-form appetizer tables with a wide and unique variety of cured meats, cheeses, vegetables, fruits, jams, dips, nuts, seeds, crackers, breads, and more.

Available as enclosed boxes (2–8 people) or large tables (200+ people)

Starts at $15 per person
